Ten problems Okta actually solves

01 / 10

Single sign-on

Employees juggle a different login for every app.

One authentication at Okta issues a session every connected app trusts, so a user signs in once and moves between tools without re-entering credentials.

02 / 10

Adaptive multi-factor authentication

A correct password isn't proof the right person is typing it.

Okta scores context — device, location, network reputation — at sign-in, and only interrupts the user with a second factor when that score crosses a risk threshold.

Okta VerifyWebAuthnRisk Signal

03 / 10

Lifecycle management

Manual account creation and revocation is slow, and offboarding gets missed.

An HR event — hire, transfer, termination — flows into Okta, which provisions or deprovisions the matching accounts across every downstream app automatically.

SCIMHRIS-drivenJML

04 / 10

Universal directory

Identity data is scattered and inconsistent across systems of record.

Okta ingests identities from Active Directory, the HR system and other sources into one mastered profile per person, so every app reads from a single, current record.

AD ImportProfile Mastering

05 / 10

API access management

APIs need a scalable way to verify a caller's identity and permissions.

A client gets a token from Okta's authorization server; the API validates that token's signature and scopes locally against Okta's published keys, with no callback on every request.

OAuth2JWTJWKS

06 / 10

Customer identity (CIAM)

Customer-facing apps need frictionless signup without owning password security.

Auth0 — Okta's customer identity platform — hosts login and signup, including social and passwordless options, then hands the app a token, scaling to millions of end users.

Auth0Social LoginPasswordless

07 / 10

B2B partner federation

A partner wants their staff to use their own credentials, not a new account you manage.

Okta federates trust with a partner's identity provider over SAML or OIDC, so their employees authenticate at their own IdP and arrive pre-verified — no shared passwords, no duplicate accounts.

SAMLOIDC FederationIdP-to-IdP

08 / 10

Zero trust / device-bound access

A correct password from an untrusted device is still a risk.

Okta checks device posture — managed, compliant, patched — alongside user identity, and can block access from unmanaged or risky devices even when the credentials are valid.

Device TrustMDM SignalConditional Access

09 / 10

Identity governance & certification

Knowing who can log in isn't the same as knowing who still should.

Okta handles authentication while a governance platform like Saviynt manages access requests, approvals and periodic certification — access that isn't recertified is revoked back through Okta automatically.

IGASoDAccess Certification

10 / 10

Non-human & AI agent identity

AI agents and service accounts are a new class of caller that also needs governing.

An agent authenticates with its own client credentials, receives a narrowly scoped, short-lived token for exactly the call it needs to make, and every action it takes is logged under its own identity.

Client CredentialsToken ExchangeNon-Human ID
